About

I’m Shane. I stay with the business as it changes.

I started as a VA in 2022 with one founder — still with her. We went from Shopify to Amazon to GoHighLevel (site, funnels, emails, courses) and social. Before that I was an executive assistant and assistant project manager. When a CEO wanted a new venture standing up, I did the research and setup.

I’m founder-led, not an agency pitch. You tell me what’s stuck. We pick one lane. If it’s outside what I should own, I’ll say so.

Who is this for?

Coaches, consultants, agents, and small service businesses that are still doing their own follow-ups and admin.

Do I need to know what to delegate?

No. That’s what the 15-minute chat is for. We pick one clear lane for the trial.

Can you help me start something new?

Yes. If you want to enter a new venture and feel lost in the setup, I take the messy research and get a first version standing — site, funnel, listing, or ops — then we decide what continues.

Is this an agency?

It’s a startup, founder-led service. I do the work. I can match extra help later if we both want that.

Where are you based?

Philippines. I work with clients in the US, UK, and Australia around overlapping hours.
